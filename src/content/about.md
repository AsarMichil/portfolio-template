# About Me

I'm a final year Computer Science student at UBC, originally from Toronto and now enjoying life in Vancouver. I like building software that _feels_ nice to use, whether that's a smooth interface or a backend that just works.

Off the laptop I'm usually biking around the city, trying a new coffee shop or at a concert. Toronto and Vancouver both have great music scenes, from underground electronic to indie rock.

I'm optimistic about Canadian tech. I've worked at startups here and seen how much a small team can do, and I think we have what it takes to compete globally.
